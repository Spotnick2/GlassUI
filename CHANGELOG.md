# Changelog

## v0.1.0

The first release. Its alphas preview it.

- **Blizzard's windows in liquid glass:** the merchant, the bags, the Escape menu, Guild &
  Communities, the character window, the world map and quest log, the loot window and `/loot`'s
  roll history, the trade window, the gossip window, the NPC quest dialog and the quest details
  popup, the damage meter, the objective tracker, and the breath, fatigue and feign death
  timers. Blizzard's windows keep working underneath; only their look changes, and each skin has
  its own switch.
- **RestedXP's guide window and waypoint arrow in glass**, when RestedXP is installed: the window
  in smoky glass with each step on its own card, and an arrow in the suite accent over a smoky
  distance pill. Two switches, `rxp` and `rxparrow`.
- **The suite theme, Pearl:** one glass for the family. Glass Unit Frames and Glass Raid Frames
  follow it if you let them (`/glassui suite`).
- **The suite look:** clear, smoked or class-colour glass (`/glassui theme`), and how buttons
  answer the mouse, on the "Suite look" settings page (`/glassui look`).
- **Light quest text on glass:** an option that turns on Blizzard's Dark quest text, so quest
  pages read well on glass, and gives your own setting back when you turn it off
  (`/glassui questtext on|off`).
- **Commands:** `/glassui skins` lists every skin and whether it's on; `/glassui skin <name> on|off`
  and `/glassui on|off` switch them at once, without a /reload. `/glassui help` lists the rest.
- **Steps aside** for the gamepad interface; for ElvUI, EllesmereUI and Tukui, which skin these
  windows too; for bag addons (Bagnon, Baganator, AdiBags, ArkInventory, BetterBags, EllesmereUI's
  bags); for Leatrix Maps' simple map; and for Glass Maps and Glass Quest Tracker, which own their
  windows.
- **One home for the Glass addons:** their settings under one "Glass UI" entry, and a page listing
  the family. Every Glass addon still works without Glass UI.
