# Changelog

## v0.1.0

Not released yet.

- **Blizzard's windows in liquid glass**, on LibGlass r8's clean material: the merchant, popups,
  the character sheet and the world map. Blizzard's windows keep working underneath; only their
  look changes, and each skin has its own switch.
- **Three looks:** clear, smoked and class-colour glass (`/glassui theme`).
- **Commands:** `/glassui skins` lists every skin and whether it's on; `/glassui skin <name> on|off`
  and `/glassui on|off` switch them, most of them without a /reload.
- **Steps aside** for the gamepad interface, and for ElvUI, EllesmereUI and Tukui, which skin
  these windows too.
- **One home for the Glass addons:** their settings under one "Glass UI" entry, and a page listing
  the family. Every Glass addon still works without Glass UI.
